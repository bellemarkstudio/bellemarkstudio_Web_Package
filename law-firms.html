What is in the setup

Every part of the system, in plain terms.

Each firm gets a custom setup built around how it takes in new matters. This is what it can include.

01

AI receptionist

Answers your phone in your firm's voice, every call, all day.

  • Answers all calls, or only the ones you miss, after hours or during court
  • Says it is an automated assistant, never pretends to be a person
  • Follows your intake script: name, number, reason for calling, how urgent, best time to call back
  • Answers common questions you approve, like hours, practice areas and location
  • Does not give legal advice or promise outcomes, ever
  • Ends silent calls and spam so they do not eat your minutes
  • Points real emergencies to 911 with wording you approve
02

Urgency sorting and instant alerts

The call is sorted the moment it ends, and the right person hears about it.

  • Each call is marked urgent, new potential client, message for an attorney or general
  • Alerts go to your email and phone, to whoever you choose
  • Urgent matters are flagged in the subject line so you see them first
  • Every alert has the name, number, reason, a short summary and a link to the lead
03

Lead pipeline and CRM

Every inquiry lands in one place, not on a sticky note.

  • Each caller is saved as a lead with their details and what they said
  • Call recordings and transcripts attached to the lead
  • Stages for new, contacted, consultation booked and signed
  • See at a glance who has been called back and who has not
04

Missed call text back

If a caller hangs up before they are helped, they hear from you in seconds.

  • An automatic text goes out right after a missed or dropped call
  • Their reply comes back to your inbox and is saved to the lead
  • Text messaging is registered with carriers so messages are delivered
05

Website chat assistant

The same intake, for visitors who would rather type than call.

  • Answers common questions on your site, any hour
  • Collects the visitor's details and the reason they reached out
  • Sends you the same alert and saves the same lead record
  • Follows the same rules: automated assistant, no legal advice
06

Automatic follow up

New inquiries do not go cold while you are busy.

  • Follow up texts and emails to new inquiries, in wording you approve
  • Stops when the person replies or books
  • You decide what is sent and when
07

Website and business email

A website that matches your firm, with email on your own domain.

  • A website built around your practice areas and your voice
  • Chat assistant and a contact form built in
  • Email addresses on your firm's domain
08

Review requests

Ask for reviews when a matter closes, without remembering to.

  • A review request goes out automatically after a matter is closed
  • Goes only to the clients you choose
  • More reviews help the next caller choose you

Questions

Why are prices a range?

A solo attorney taking a few calls a week needs less than a five attorney firm covering several practice areas. We quote after the demo, in writing, and the standard numbers on this page are the likely maximum.

What happens after the first 3 months of the holiday price?

The monthly moves to $1,400. It is written in your agreement and we tell you before you sign, so there are no surprises.

What if we go over the included minutes or texts?

Extra call minutes are $0.25 each and extra texts are $0.03 each. Most small firms stay well inside the included amounts.

Will it ever say it is a person?

No. It identifies itself as an automated assistant and does not give legal advice.

Can we change what it says?

Yes. You approve the wording before launch and we make changes for you afterward.
