Traffic and license

Super Speeder: what that extra fee really costs

The state fee is only the first bill. The part that catches people is what happens if the notice goes to an old address.

August 11, 2026Traffic and licenseAbout 4 min

Two bills, not one

Georgia's Super Speeder law adds a state fee on top of whatever the court charges, for drivers convicted of going 75 or more on a two lane road or 85 or more anywhere. It is not part of the ticket you pay at the courthouse. It arrives later, from the state, as a separate notice.

People pay the court, assume they are finished, and throw away the second letter.

What happens when it goes unpaid

The state suspends the license. Not for speeding, for the unpaid fee. And because the notice goes to whatever address the Department of Driver Services has on file, drivers who moved and never updated it often find out the way you least want to find out, during a traffic stop.

The expensive version of this is never the fee. It is the suspended license charge you pick up six months later because a letter went to an old apartment.

The real cost

  • The court fine on the underlying citation.
  • The state fee on top of it.
  • A reinstatement fee if the license was suspended for nonpayment.
  • Insurance. A serious speeding conviction follows you at renewal for years, and that number is usually the biggest one on this list.
  • A second charge if you drive on the suspended license, which is a misdemeanor, not a ticket.

What can be done before it gets there

The fee attaches to a conviction. No conviction, no fee. That is why what happens at the courthouse matters more than people think for a speeding charge in this range. Depending on the court and your record, the charge can sometimes be reduced below the Super Speeder threshold, which removes the state fee entirely.

Once you have paid the citation, you have pled guilty and that option is gone.

Two things to do today

Update your address with the Department of Driver Services, and do it before you need it. Then, if you have a pending citation in this range, talk to someone before the due date rather than after.
