Wills and estates in Newnan, Georgia

Plain paperwork now saves your family a year of court later.

Three documents cover most families

You do not need a complicated plan to protect a normal family. Most of our clients need the same three things, done properly and signed with the right witnesses.

  • A will. Who gets what, and who is in charge of making it happen.
  • A financial power of attorney. Someone who can pay your bills and deal with your bank if you cannot.
  • A healthcare directive. Who speaks for you about treatment, and what you want when you cannot say it.

What happens without a will

Georgia decides for you. The intestacy statute splits the estate by formula, not by what anyone intended, and a judge appoints the administrator. For blended families, unmarried partners and anyone with a small business, the formula result is usually not what the person would have chosen.

The cost of doing nothing is never zero. It is just paid later, by the people you leave behind.

Probate

When someone dies, their estate usually has to go through probate in the county where they lived. We handle that start to finish for families: the petition, the notices, the inventory, creditor claims and the final distribution.

Guardianship

When an adult can no longer manage their own affairs and there is no power of attorney in place, guardianship or conservatorship is the court route. It is slower and more public than planning ahead, which is exactly why we push the planning.
