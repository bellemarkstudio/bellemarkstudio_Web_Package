Criminal defense in Newnan, Georgia

From a first misdemeanor to a serious felony charge, you want someone in the room early.

Early matters more than it sounds

By the time most people call a lawyer, the state has had a head start. Officers have written reports, witnesses have given statements, and a file is already moving. Getting counsel involved before the first court date changes what is still possible.

In some cases that means the charge is reduced before it is ever formally filed. In others it means a warrant is handled on our terms, with a bond already arranged, instead of during a traffic stop six weeks later.

What we handle

  • Misdemeanors. Simple battery, shoplifting, possession of marijuana, criminal trespass, disorderly conduct.
  • Felonies. Drug distribution, burglary, aggravated assault, theft by taking over the felony threshold.
  • Probation violations. Technical violations and new arrests, including warrants already issued.
  • Pre arrest representation. When you know an investigation is open but no charge has been filed yet.

Honest answers about outcomes

No attorney can promise a result, and you should be careful with anyone who does. What we can tell you early is the realistic range: what similar cases have done in this circuit, what the state's evidence actually proves, and where the weak points are.

Our job in the first meeting is to replace a worst case running through your head with the real range of outcomes.

If someone is in custody right now

Call. Tell our assistant it is urgent and give the county and the person's name. That goes to an attorney flagged as high priority, day or night. First appearance usually happens within 48 hours, and being ready for it matters.
