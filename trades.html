Questions

Quick answers.

Is the assistant a real person?

No. It is an automated assistant that answers common questions and collects the details of the problem. It never makes promises for you, and a real person follows up on every request.

Will this get me more customers?

We cannot promise that. We do not promise leads or rankings. We make sure people who reach you get an answer fast, at any hour, and that no lead gets lost.

What does the 3 month minimum mean?

You agree to at least 3 months so we have time to set up and tune everything. After that it continues month to month and you can cancel any time with written notice before your next billing date. If you cancel during the first 3 months, you pay half of the monthly fees for the months that are left.

Who owns the site and the domain?

Bellemark owns the website design, code, hosting and domain while your service is active. You own your business name, logo, photos, text and customer information. If you cancel, the domain transfers to you for a flat $25 fee, and we keep your site running during the transfer.

What if the phone carriers reject the texting?

Texting needs approval from carriers, which we do not control. The Tier 2 agreement explains what happens if it is rejected, and the texting part of the fee does not start until your business number is working.

What is not included?

Paid ads, search engine work, photography, large amounts of new content, extra pages and redesigns. Anything outside the package is quoted first, so you always see the price before we start.

How do I reach you?

Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm Eastern, by message or call. We reply within 1 business day. Change requests go through the change request form.

What happens with my customers' information?

It belongs to you. We use it only to run your system. Our privacy policy explains how we handle it.
