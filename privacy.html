1. Who this covers

This policy explains how Bellemark Studio ("Bellemark," "we," "us") handles information on this website and when you contact us, book a demo or become a customer. We are a business based in Newnan, Georgia. We build websites, phone answering and lead management systems for local service businesses and law firms.

This policy covers two groups of people. First, visitors and prospects who contact us. Second, the customers and callers of our clients, whose information passes through systems we set up for our clients. Section 6 explains that second group.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, business name and your role.
  • What you tell us in emails, texts, calls and demos, such as what your business does, how many calls you miss and what you want built.
  • Customer information: business details, logos, photos, hours, service areas and similar material you send so we can build your system.
  • Billing information needed to invoice you. We do not ask you to send card numbers by email or text.

Information collected automatically

  • When you visit this website, the host and your browser exchange standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages requested and the time. Our hosting providers may keep logs of this for security and operations.
  • Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

Calls and texts

  • If you call or text us, we receive your phone number and what you say or write. We take notes on sales and demo calls. We do not record a call with you unless we tell you first.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information from website visitors, such as government ID numbers, bank or card numbers, or health information. Please do not send it to us.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your questions, schedule and run demos, and send quotes and agreements.
  • To build, host, maintain and support the websites and systems you hire us for.
  • To bill you and keep business records.
  • To keep our website and systems secure, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax reporting and responding to lawful requests.
  • To follow up about our services. You can tell us to stop at any time (see section 10).

We do not use your information to train artificial intelligence models, and we do not sell it.

4. Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and ask us to contact you, we may text you about your inquiry, your demo, your quote or your service. Message frequency varies with your conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts from us and HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of buying anything from us.

We do not share your mobile number or text opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers that deliver our texts, such as our messaging platform and phone carriers, handle your number only to deliver messages for us.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with the following, and only as needed:

  • Service providers that help us run the business: email, document and spreadsheet tools (Google), our customer relationship, phone, texting and AI platform (HighLevel), and website hosting providers (such as GitHub and Netlify). They may process your information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants or lawyers, under duties of confidentiality.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety or property.
  • A buyer or successor if Bellemark is sold or merges, with notice to you where required.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross context behavioral advertising.

6. Information we handle for our clients

When a business or law firm hires us, our systems answer calls, collect inquiries and store contact records for that client. The information belongs to the client. In that role we act as the client's service provider: we handle the information only to provide the service, on the client's instructions, and we do not use it for our own marketing or for any other client.

If you called or texted one of our clients and want to see, correct or delete your information, please contact that business directly. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to them.

For law firm clients, additional ethics and confidentiality rules apply to the firm. Our approach is described on our compliance page. Before launch we give each client a written summary of which vendors process their data, where it is stored, who can access it, how long it is kept and how it is deleted when the service ends.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, and it does not run analytics or tracking tools. If that changes, we will update this policy first and add a notice where the law requires one. Your browser may still store ordinary technical data needed to display the pages.

8. How long we keep it

We keep prospect and business records for as long as they are useful for the relationship, and then for as long as we need them for tax, legal and accounting reasons. Contact details from someone who never becomes a customer are deleted on request, or when we no longer need them. Client system data is kept while the service is active and then returned or deleted as the client's agreement says.

9. How we protect it

We use reputable providers, limit access to the people who need it, and keep sign in details out of shared documents. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of unauthorized access to information we hold for you, we will tell you promptly so you can act.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • You can tell us to stop emailing, calling or texting you. Reply STOP to a text or email us.
  • Depending on where you live, such as California and several other states, you may have additional rights, including to know, correct, delete and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we will not treat you differently for exercising a right.

To make a request, email bellemarkcontact@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it is you, and we will answer within 45 days.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

12. Other websites

This website may link to other sites, including sample sites and agreement pages. We do not control those sites and this policy does not apply to them.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so on this page or contact you.
