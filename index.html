Straight answers

What it is, and what it is not.

It is an AI, not a person

Callers are told they are speaking with an automated assistant. A real person at your firm follows up on every inquiry.

It does not give legal advice

It answers calls, collects contact details and a short reason for the call, and routes it. It does not advise anyone about their case.

No promises about results

We do not promise cases, revenue or rankings. We make sure the people who call you get answered.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can I control what the receptionist says?

Yes. You approve the greeting, the questions it asks and how it handles each kind of call before it goes live. Changes to what it says count as change requests.

What happens with call recording and client information?

We set up any recording notice you need and keep the setup to the contact details and short reason for the call. Your firm decides what is collected. Bring your compliance questions to the demo and we will go through them with you.

Will it ever say it is a person?

No. It identifies itself as an automated assistant if asked.

What if a caller is in a real emergency?

Urgent calls are flagged and sent to you right away. We recommend a message directing true emergencies to 911 and we will set that up with you.

How much does it cost?

Custom setups are quoted for your firm after the demo, in writing, so you only pay for what you need.

Do you also build websites?

Yes. A website with a chat assistant can be part of the setup, or you can start with a smaller package. See the website and lead packages.
