Questions

Quick answers.

Do I need to do anything after I sign up?

You send your services, towns, hours and any photos you have. We build the rest.

How do I ask for a change?

Fill out the change request form any time. You get 3 changes every month. Standard changes are done within 3 business days. Priority changes are done within 1 business day for $20 each. Changes to what the assistant says count too.

Is the chat assistant a real person?

No. It is an automated assistant that answers common questions and points visitors to your form. A real person follows up on every form request.

Will this get me more customers?

We cannot promise that. We do not promise leads or rankings. We make sure people who find you can get answers fast, at any hour.

What does the 3 month minimum mean?

You agree to at least 3 months so we have time to set up and tune everything. After that it continues month to month and you can cancel any time with written notice before your next billing date. The full terms are in a written agreement you see before you pay.

What if I cancel early?

If you cancel during the first 3 months, you pay half of the monthly fees for the months that are left. The month you cancel in is already paid.

What if a payment is missed?

We contact you several times first, by message, email and phone. The full schedule and the fees are in the agreement you read before you pay.

Who owns the site and the domain?

Bellemark owns the website design, the code, the hosting and the domain while your service is active. You own your business name, logo, photos, text and customer information. If you cancel, the domain transfers to you for a flat $25 fee, and we keep your site running during the transfer so it does not go dark.

What is not included?

Paid ads, search engine work, large amounts of new content, photography, extra pages and a redesign are not included. Business email, a customer database and texting are included in Tier 2.

How do I reach you?

Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm Eastern, by message or call. We reply within 1 business day. Change requests go through the change request form.

Can I read the full agreement first?

Yes. Read the Tier 1 agreement or the Tier 2 agreement before you pay anything. They are short and in plain English.

What is the difference between Tier 1 and Tier 2?

Tier 1 is a website with a chat assistant that answers common questions, for $500 once and $150 a month. Tier 2 adds a CRM, business email, a business number with text alerts, missed call text back with an AI assistant, and review requests, for $1,350 once and $650 a month.

Can I get a custom package?

Yes. Custom packages are quoted for your business, and the price is negotiable. Contact us with a custom inquiry and tell us what you need.

What about bigger changes like a new page?

A new page, a new section or a redesign is quoted separately and starts at $250. We always give you the price first.
