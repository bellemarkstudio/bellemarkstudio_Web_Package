RH

Rachel Hollis

Founding partner

Rachel started Brightwater in 2011 after four years as an assistant district attorney in the Coweta Judicial Circuit. She knows how the other side builds a DUI case because she used to build them.

She takes a small number of cases at a time so clients get her cell number, not a message slip. Most of her work is DUI and serious traffic, with criminal defense alongside it.

Daniel Okafor

Partner

Daniel spent five years as a public defender and tried more cases before thirty than most lawyers try in a career. He joined Brightwater in 2016 and leads the firm’s felony work.

He is the person clients call at 2 a.m. when a family member is in custody, and he is direct about what a case is really worth.

MB

Maya Brennan

Attorney, family law

Maya handles the firm’s divorce and custody work. She is a registered neutral and settles most of her cases without a trial, which usually saves clients both money and a year of their life.

When a case does need to be tried, she tries it. She is known for parenting plans that hold up because they were written around how a family actually lives.
