Questions

Before you reach out.

Am I talking to a real person?

No. The assistant on the phone and in the chat is automated, and it says so. A person at the firm follows up on every inquiry.

Can the assistant tell me if I have a case?

No. It cannot give legal advice, tell you whether you have a case, or promise anything about what the firm will do. It can pass your details to an attorney.

What should I share in the chat?

Only a short reason for contacting us, plus your name and contact details. Please do not share confidential details until you speak with an attorney.

What if this is an emergency?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 at any time.
