Personal injury in Newnan, Georgia

If someone else caused your injury, the insurer is already building its file. You should have one too.

The insurer started before you did

Within a day or two of a wreck, an adjuster has a file open, a recorded statement request ready and a number in mind. They are good at their job. The first call usually sounds helpful and is not.

You are not required to give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer, and doing it early rarely helps you.

What we do first

  • Preserve what disappears. Dash and business camera footage is often overwritten within days. A letter goes out immediately.
  • Get you treated. Gaps in treatment are the single most common thing used to reduce a claim.
  • Find every policy. The at fault driver, their employer if they were working, and your own uninsured and underinsured coverage.
  • Track the real cost. Not just bills. Missed shifts, mileage to appointments, and what you can no longer do.

Fees

Injury cases are handled on a contingency fee, which means no fee unless there is a recovery. The percentage and how case expenses are handled are written into the agreement before any work starts, in plain language.

Time limits

Georgia's statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is two years, and shorter notice deadlines apply when a city or county is involved. Those deadlines are hard. Waiting to see if you feel better is the most expensive thing people do.

The strongest claims are not the most dramatic ones. They are the best documented ones.
